Privacy Policy

How Audio Switcher handles information on your device.

Last updated: 17 November 2026
Privacy at a glance
  • No account required
  • Information is processed locally
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking
  • No microphone recording permission

Audio Switcher provides audio output controls, audio testing, and optional listening features.

Information we collect

Audio Switcher does not require an account and does not collect or send personal information to a server. The current project does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Information used on your device

The app uses information about available audio outputs, media volume, and proximity sensor state to provide its features. This information is processed locally on your device.

Your selected settings and temporary audio restoration information may be stored locally on your Android device. Android backup is disabled for the app.

Permissions

The app uses audio settings access to provide audio controls. On Android, it may show a notification while an audio routing session is active.

If you enable the floating control, the app requests permission to display over other apps. You can revoke this permission in your device settings.

Microphone and audio

Audio Switcher does not request microphone recording permission. It does not record calls, music, or audio from other apps. Test tones and silent audio used for routing are generated locally.

Third-party services

The current project does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. This policy should be updated if such services are added.
