Privacy Policy

How EV Charge handles location, searches and saved stations.

Effective November 5, 2026 · Last updated November 5, 2026
Privacy at a glance
  • Location permission is optional
  • Favorites are saved locally
  • No account required
  • Station and map requests use third-party services

EV Charge (“the app”) is provided by Code Micros (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how the app uses information when you find electric vehicle charging stations.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at codemicros@gmail.com.

Information the app uses

Location

If you grant location permission, EV Charge can access your device’s location to find charging stations near you. Depending on your device settings and permission choice, this may include precise or approximate location.

The app sends the coordinates used for a nearby search to Open Charge Map to retrieve charging station results. If you select a city or search a map area instead, the app sends coordinates for that selected area.

Location permission is optional. You can use city search and browse supported locations without granting access to your device’s location. You can change or revoke the permission in your device settings.

Searches

When you submit a city or place search, the app sends your search text and selected country to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim search service to find a matching location. Avoid entering sensitive personal information in the search field.

Favorites and settings

The app stores your saved charging stations and selected country locally on your device. The current version does not require an account or provide cloud synchronization for these settings.

Technical information

When the app connects to a service, that service may receive technical information needed to handle the request, such as your IP address, request details, and an app identification string. Map tile requests also reveal the map areas being viewed.

How information is used and shared

The app uses the information described above to show maps, search for locations, retrieve nearby charging stations, remember your preferences, and open directions or station information when requested.

The app connects directly to these services:

  • Open Charge Map provides charging station information. It receives the coordinates, search radius and country used for a station request, along with network and request information. Learn more at Open Charge Map.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation services provide map tiles and place search through Nominatim. These services receive the relevant map tile requests or submitted search text, along with network and request information. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps or your browser may open when you request directions. The selected charging station’s coordinates are included in the directions link. The service you open handles information under its own privacy policy.
  • Open Charge Map’s website may open if you choose to view a station’s source listing. The link includes the station’s identifier.

These providers may process or retain information under their own policies. Contact us if you need help identifying the relevant provider for a privacy request.

The current app does not include an account, advertising SDK or analytics service. We do not use the information handled by the app to sell personal information or to show targeted advertisements.

Data retention and deletion

Your selected country and favorites remain stored on your device until you change or remove them, clear the app’s data through your device settings, or uninstall the app. Device backup and restore settings may affect whether local data is restored.

The app uses location and search results to provide the requested feature. It does not provide an account or a developer-operated cloud database for storing your favorites or location history.

Third-party services may keep network or request logs under their own retention policies. We cannot promise deletion of records controlled by those providers. You may contact us at codemicros@gmail.com for help with a deletion request, or contact the relevant provider directly.

Security

The app uses HTTPS for its default connections to Open Charge Map, OpenStreetMap services and external web links. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device with its available security settings.

Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Grant, change or revoke location permission in your device settings.
  • Search by city or browse a supported location without allowing device location access.
  • Remove individual favorites in the app.
  • Clear the app’s local data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored settings and favorites.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to access, correct or delete it. To make a request or ask about your rights, contact codemicros@gmail.com. We may need information to understand and respond to your request.

Children’s privacy

EV Charge is intended for people looking for electric vehicle charging stations and is not specifically directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a privacy enquiry or another interaction with us, contact codemicros@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or our privacy practices change. We will update the date at the top of this page when we make changes.
